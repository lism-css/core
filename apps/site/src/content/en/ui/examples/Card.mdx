## Examples

### Card link with automatic vertical/horizontal layout switching

By building with [`WithSide`](/en/docs/primitives/l--withSide/), the layout automatically switches to vertical when the display area becomes narrow.

Additionally, [`is--boxLink`](/en/docs/trait-class/is--boxLink/) is applied to make the entire card content a link.
